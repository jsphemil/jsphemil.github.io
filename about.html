About

Hi, I'm Emil.

By day I'm a manager — I lead people and keep work moving. By night I'm an indie developer, building small, calm apps that solve problems in my own life and, hopefully, yours too.

The story

Building is how I learn.

I wrote my first web page in 2020 and never really stopped. What began as evening exercises turned into a habit: find something that annoys me, then build the tool I wish existed.

That's how a fuel log became FuelTrack, how a spreadsheet of accounts became Erebor, and how my paper bullet journal became Dotgrid. I care about privacy, so most of what I make is local-first — your data stays on your device.

Managing teams taught me to keep things simple and finish what I start. Building on my own taught me everything else.
