Erebor Wealth Management

One calm place for every account you own — and none of it ever leaves your phone.

Status
v1 feature-complete
Platform
iOS & Android
Role
Solo — product, design, build
Year
2026

What it is

Local-first personal finance for iOS & Android.

Erebor is a personal finance app that tracks money across every kind of account — bank accounts, credit cards, cash, savings and investments — and shows where it all goes.

It is built local-first: there is no backend and no sign-up. Everything lives in an on-device SQLite database, so the app is fast, private and works fully offline.

It started life as a web app and was rebuilt from scratch for mobile, carrying over the lessons learned and wrapping them in a dark, focused design system named after the Lonely Mountain's vault.

Features

What it does.

  • Every account, one balance

    Bank, credit card, cash, savings and investment accounts with running balances and net worth.

  • Fast transactions

    Income, expenses and transfers with categories, tags and notes — quick to add, easy to search.

  • Spending summary & budgets

    See where money goes by category and month, then switch on Budget Mode to keep each category in check.

  • Commitments & funds

    Track recurring bills and subscriptions, and set money aside in dedicated funds for what's coming.

  • Home-screen widgets

    Quick-add, monthly cash flow and net worth, right on your home screen.

  • Private by design

    Fully offline, biometric app lock, multi-currency with a base currency, and CSV export whenever you want your data.
