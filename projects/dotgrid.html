Dotgrid Bullet Journal

Not a to-do list with a journal bolted on — Ryder Carroll's actual method, kept deliberately small.

What it is

A bullet journal for Android that makes you decide.

Dotgrid is a bullet journal for Android that implements the analog method faithfully instead of turning it into yet another task manager.

Most apps silently roll unfinished tasks into today. That feels helpful but quietly ruins the method — nothing is ever consciously abandoned. Dotgrid walks you through what's left, one item at a time, and asks you to choose. The friction is the point.

It is black and white because in this method the symbols carry the meaning, and private because your journal is a database on your phone — nothing more.

Features

What it does.

  • It makes you decide

    Leftover tasks are reviewed one by one: push forward, schedule for later, or strike out.

  • The past stays honest

    Migrating a task never rewrites history — the original stays marked “>” where it was, forever.

  • Black & white

    Dot, cross, arrow. Colour appears only where it does a job: collections, tags and calendar sources.

  • No streaks, no scores

    Plain counts only. No percentages, no charts — your journal is not a scoreboard.

  • No account, no server

    No analytics, no telemetry. Optional backups to Drive or Dropbox are encrypted on-device first.

  • Calendar aware

    Events from your calendars sit alongside your daily log, clearly marked by source.
